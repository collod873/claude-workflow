## Problems

### #1108 held 16 h 37 min with nothing to wake it

Woken at 01:32 from fault #1317, the build job branched from main (`tickets.yml:150`), rebuilt the
fix, and Save's push was refused because the branch already held it. The builder reset to the
remote branch, so its local head moved, and `checkingAgain` marks `checking` whenever the local
head moved (`src/builder.ts:360`) and logs "green and pushed" (`:370`). Nothing was pushed, no
`synchronize` came, and nothing wakes a `checking` ticket whose required check is red: no workflow
has a `schedule`. The owner found it by asking, 16 h 34 min in. `d6f6de0d` now re-runs an open
PR's failed checks on the wake, so this path needs a build with no open PR to recur; the false
"pushed" and the missing clock are unchanged.

#1090 waited 18.7 min the same way: its builder filed fault #1313, the machine refused to build a
fault filed from a split piece (fixed in `c1966615`), and the owner fixed it by hand. He found
that stall by asking too; none of the four @mentions in the window said a ticket was stuck, and
the session-end readers dropped both findings as agent-only (`cold-filings-2d160732.txt:9`,
`cold-filings-b5649c8a.txt:15` in `~/.claude/logs`).

### The review gate ends green without judging (6 of 9 Spec PRs)

8 of 17 Spec reviews logged "writes nothing, a newer run judges" and ended green; a ninth lost its
verdict to a failed read (since fixed). Five sampled logs each show `StructuredOutput
{"verdict":"drift"` before the stand-down: #1088, #1095, #1096, #1099, #1105. Eight raced the PR's
first meter write, which moves the fingerprint (`src/reviewer.ts:36-39`) by adding meter lines;
`movedUnder` (`:281-290`) stands down and an undefined stop exits 0 (`src/stops.ts:23`). The ninth,
#1093's, came from a newline: the meter reviewer reads the body with `--jq .body` untrimmed
(`src/meter-reviewer.ts:73`) and writes it back, so every write adds one. An App edit starts no
run (`.github/caller.yml:10`), so the newer run never comes, and the meter writes also defeat the
reviewer's reuse of its own past verdict (`src/reviewer.ts:322-331`).

What the dropped verdicts named, all checked against Lumaria main:

  wave #1098 cost 21.9 min and $1.32.

  about eight more accounting and crm files: after 20:00 Eastern an invoice due today shows past
  due. #1098's own Done when ("no UTC today left") is false.

### The memory guard kills runner jobs first

At 20:49:54 earlyoom killed builders #1083 and #1082 (46 and 40 MiB), then, one second later, the
owner's 3.4 GB vitest that had filled memory; it also killed eslint and vitest in their repairs.
GitHub's runner sets `oom_score_adj 500` on every job process, and claude-workflow writes the
earlyoom config itself (`src/runner.ts:33-34`), its test asserting runner jobs die before a
session. Killing 86 MiB freed nothing. The runner slices cap memory at 10 GB each on a 24 GB host
shared with the owner's sessions.
