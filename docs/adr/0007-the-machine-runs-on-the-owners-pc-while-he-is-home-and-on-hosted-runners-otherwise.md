# The machine runs on the owner's PC while he is home, and on hosted runners otherwise

The machine runs in GitHub Actions, and the owner follows Specs and tickets in the GitHub app.
Which runner takes a repo's jobs is his switch (`bin/runner`): his PC while it is on and he is home,
GitHub's standard hosted runners otherwise. Every ruling holds on both. Speed comes from the machine
doing only what is needed, not from the runner. Lumaria stays private, overage minutes are paid, and
the owner watches minutes himself. No session raises runners, minutes or cost again, in any form.

**Rejected: larger paid runners.** They buy speed the machine should earn.

**Rejected: a spending cap, alert or minutes monitor.** Machinery for a question the owner settled.

**Rejected: making Lumaria public for free minutes.** Public code cannot be taken back.

**Superseded on 2026-10-09: hosted runners only.** The owner runs Lumaria on his PC to spend fewer
minutes, and was tired of being asked about it.

Evidence: `docs/research/owner-rules-2026-10.md`, N9 and C3; the owner, 2026-10-09.
