# Agents draft vocabulary and rulings; the owner signs them

Recorded 2026-08-23.

`GOAL.md` named vocabulary as one of three places the human deliberately stays, citing W5 and two
independent rulings. The record does not support it. Across 34 ADRs and two glossaries inside the
surviving transcript window, the owner originated 3; the other 31 were proposed by an agent and
assented to, roughly a third landing with no human keystroke near them. **There is no instance of a
proposed ADR or glossary term being rejected or rewritten.** Twice an agent flagged its own work as
probably wrong and invited a reversal — `stage` filed under `_Avoid_`, and the `connector`
definition called out as "doing argument-shaped work before the argument happens." Neither was
answered; both still stand as written.

W5 is therefore restated: **agents draft, the owner signs.** Vocabulary is struck from the list of
places the human deliberately stays.

## Considered options

- **Keep the boundary and accept the click is ceremonial.** Rejected: it leaves the charter
  describing a wall nothing has ever found, which makes every other line in that section harder to
  trust.
- **Strike the whole boundary.** Rejected: one of the three items is load-bearing and the record
  proves it. [ADR-0002](0002-work-executes-on-github-hosted-runners-never-on-the-workstat.md) is a
  destination call where the owner overrode a recommendation hard — *"I DONT WANT IT ON MY
  COMPUTER"* — and that override became the ruling. Visual and spatial verdicts are supported
  separately by agent-skills #127.

## Consequences

The signature has to carry something, or this becomes a rubber stamp with better paperwork. Across
81 menu-style questions in the same window the owner took the recommended first option 73 times —
but those were asked mid-work, in sessions where he had said things like *"i dont really understand
this part at all so your best judgement is good."* The decision sheet is the opposite situation: his
own idea, at the top of the pipeline, where he holds the most context of anyone.

**That is a prediction, and it is the thing to measure.** If the override rate on sheets looks like
73/81, the sheet is not giving him enough to push against, and the fault is the sheet's.
